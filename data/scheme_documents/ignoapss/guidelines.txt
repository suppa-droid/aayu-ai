Official Guidelines for Indira Gandhi National Old Age Pension Scheme (IGNOAPS):
1. Target Group: Senior citizens belonging to Below Poverty Line (BPL) households.
2. Age Criteria: Minimum age 60 years.
3. Disbursal Tiers:
   - Age 60 to 79 years: Rs. 500 per month.
   - Age 80 years and above: Rs. 800 per month.
4. Mandatory Documents: Aadhaar Card, BPL Ration Card, Bank Account Passbook, Age Proof.
